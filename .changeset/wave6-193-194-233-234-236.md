Ship Wave 6 end-to-end Scene, Wasm FFI, TypeScript SDK, Worker client, domain facade, and Showcase UI workflows:

- Interactive face selection (lasso, box, brush, crease, shell, topological grow/shrink/invert/holes/smooth/islands, mask bridge, and extract/split/delete) (#233)
- Free-form deformation engines (FFD B-spline/Bezier lattices, ARAP handles, Elastic Kelvinlets, and XPBD cloth/elastic with vertex protection and self-collision avoidance) (#193)
- Inter-object collision response modes (`passThrough`, `rigid`, `cut`, `softDeform`) integrated into interactive `drag`, `moveWithCollision`, and pair/group rules (#194)
- Geometric primitive fitting (plane, sphere, cylinder, cone), datum alignment, section contours (SVG/JSON/CSV/DXF export), and point cloud parsing/filtering/normal estimation/surface reconstruction (#234)
- SDF hollowing, chamfered drain-hole punching with removable plugs, and surface-aligned `placeMesh` stamping (#236)
