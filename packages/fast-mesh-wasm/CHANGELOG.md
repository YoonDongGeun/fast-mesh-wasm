- [#58](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/58) [`ae40f1d`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/ae40f1d042561ed663b43954fb1bf9db488c53f8) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Add `SceneStore.coloringTargets(mesh)`: the targets of the store-owned field behind a mesh's colouring, in the field's order (ascending scene id), which is the order its nearest-target indices (`fieldNearest`, `fetchFieldNearest`) refer to; frozen, or `null` without a colouring. Use it to name the partner behind a contact group member's vertex: the order differs from `distanceTo` and from the group's member list.

- [#58](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/58) [`ae40f1d`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/ae40f1d042561ed663b43954fb1bf9db488c53f8) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Add `contactHeatmap(options)` to `fast-mesh-wasm/store`: a preset distance colouring (everything but `distanceTo`) for two meshes in contact, with penetration from deep red (`depth`) to red, a white contact band across `[-tolerance, +tolerance]`, and the gap from green to blue (`gap`). `exactDepth: true` computes the whole field (`maxDistance: Infinity`) so any penetration depth shows; the default bounds it at `2 · max(depth, gap)`, past the range ends, so the coloured area's outer edge is a value boundary. `gap: 0` colours penetration only (the range is `[-depth, tolerance]`). It throws `INVALID_ARGUMENT` unless `depth > 0`, `gap >= 0` and `0 <= tolerance < min(depth, gap)` (`< depth` when `gap` is 0). A tolerance or colour change reuses the store's field.
  
  Add `SceneStore.fetchColoringValues(mesh)`: a copy of the values of the store-owned field behind a mesh's colouring, or `null` when it has none.

- [#58](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/58) [`ae40f1d`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/ae40f1d042561ed663b43954fb1bf9db488c53f8) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Add fragment shading for colourings, so the edge between coloured and uncoloured areas is crisp instead of blurred across a triangle. `bindMesh` (`fast-mesh-wasm/three`) takes `shading: "vertex" | "fragment"` (default `"vertex"`, unchanged) and `edgeSoftness` (distance units, 0 = a hard edge; `BoundMesh.setEdgeSoftness` changes it without a recompile). In `"fragment"` mode each pixel is coloured from the interpolated field value through the colouring's rule (a lookup texture and a shader chunk added with `onBeforeCompile`), so range edges and a contact heatmap's tolerance band are exact iso-lines and gradients are smooth per pixel; at a vertex the colour equals the vertex colour, and far (`Infinity`) and NaN values never tint their neighbours. `<FastMesh>` passes `shading` and `edgeSoftness` through.
  
  Add `views(mesh).values()`: the values a mesh's colours are made from (one `f32` per vertex slot, `null` without colouring), changing only at the colours' dirty ranges; `WorkerScene` mirrors them from the first `values()` read on, so vertex shading pays nothing for them. Add `coloring(mesh)` on `Scene` and `WorkerScene`: a frozen copy of the rule (the same object until the rule changes) the mesh's colours are made by (on a `WorkerScene`, the rule of the results applied so far, not a pending or rejected one). A fragment-shaded binding needs its own material; `MeshBasicMaterial` is accepted too, and other materials fall back to vertex colours with a warning. On `SceneLike` and `MeshViews` both are optional. Colours of a reused vertex slot are now reported (and recoloured from its reset value).

- [#57](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/57) [`7484962`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/7484962d301e440f73329fce48c25e25c9dadd06) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Distance fields to several meshes: `DistanceFieldDesc.to` takes a list, each vertex holding the smallest signed distance over the targets (bit-identical to the minimum of single-target fields), with `fieldNearest` / `fetchFieldNearest` telling which target each value came from and `fieldTargets` listing them. Moving one mesh recomputes only the fields on it or measured to it, and removing a target removes it from the field. `fieldStatus` sums the sign status up over the targets, and `fieldTargetStatus` gives each one. In the store, `DistanceColoring.distanceTo` takes a list too (a set: order does not matter), and `setContactGroup(id, { meshes, pairs?, coloring })` colours a group of meshes by contact with their partners.

- [#41](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/41) [`569fc9d`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/569fc9d46638fe81cade36ba6566ab31b7e7c553) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Add the `Scene` API: many meshes in one WebAssembly scene with placements, a BVH per mesh, raycast / closest-point / mesh-distance / contact queries, signed distance fields with colour maps, planar sections, edit op lists, mesh analysis and hole repair, and undo/redo with drags as one step. Render buffers (positions, normals, RGBA8 colours, indices) are read through zero-copy typed-array views, and `commitFrame()` reports the dirty ranges to upload. Errors are `FastMeshError`s with a `code`. The wasm binary grows from 19.5 KB to 733 KB (292 KB gzip).

- [#49](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/49) [`be8ecbb`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/be8ecbbb646d66ffaab04c6c8b4d31784df952de) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Add `fast-mesh-wasm/store`, `fast-mesh-wasm/react` and `fast-mesh-wasm/r3f`.
  
  `fast-mesh-wasm/store`: `createSceneStore(scene)` wraps a `Scene` or a `WorkerScene` in a `SceneStore` with frozen, structurally shared snapshots (`getSnapshot` / `subscribe`): per mesh its applied `display`, the `desired` display and a `status` (`"idle"`, `"computing"`, `"ready"` or `"error"`), plus sections, history, selection and an overall status. Besides the scene's actions it adds declarative ones: `setDisplay(mesh, { coloring, opacity, visible })`, where `coloring` colours a mesh by its distance to another through a distance field the store owns (one per coloured mesh, reused when the colouring is toggled back on), `setSection(section, { plane, meshes, fill })` and `select(mesh)`. Declarative changes apply at the next `store.commitFrame()`, the last value per frame winning, so rapid toggling never queues work; a mesh or section reads `"computing"` until its change is shown, including while a placement, edit or undo it depends on is pending. `opacity`, `visible` and the selection live only in the store and never enter history. The store has no dependencies (no React, three.js or wasm glue).
  
  `fast-mesh-wasm/react` (optional peer `react >=18`): `SceneProvider`, `useScene`, `useStoreSelector`, `useMeshDisplay`, `useSection`, `useHistory`, `useSceneStatus` and `useSelection`, built on `useSyncExternalStore` with memoised selectors, so components re-render only when their slice changes and render an empty snapshot on the server. Bind controlled inputs to `state.desired`; the top-level fields are what is shown.
  
  `fast-mesh-wasm/r3f` (optional peer `@react-three/fiber >=9`, tested with 9.x; v8 is not supported): `<FastMesh mesh />` draws a scene mesh through `bindMesh`, follows its opacity and visibility, and disposes on unmount or removal; `useFrameDriver(store)` calls `store.commitFrame()` once per rendered frame however many `<FastMesh>` are mounted.
  
  A new example, `examples/react`, runs the store on a Worker scene with colouring toggles, opacity, visibility, a section, undo/redo, Restart and TransformControls dragging.

- [#52](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/52) [`4b6111e`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/4b6111ee1250c088a9b9c2e23fa528c2b74b6f84) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Multithreaded Worker scene: on a cross-origin isolated page (COOP `same-origin` and COEP `require-corp` headers), `fast-mesh-wasm/worker` now runs a threaded build of the engine, its WebAssembly on a pool of Workers sharing one memory, with results bit-identical to the single-threaded engine. On a wide-range distance-field drag of two 1M-triangle meshes, 8 threads make a recompute about 1.6 to 4.5 times faster and halve the longest Worker block (headless Chrome). New option `createWorkerScene({ threads })`: the pool size, by default the logical cores at most 8, clamped to 1 to 64; `threads: 1` runs the single-threaded engine and never fetches the threaded files. New getter `WorkerScene.threads` tells how many threads the engine computes with. On a page that is not isolated, or when the threaded engine cannot start (import, instantiation, shared memory, or a thread pool that fails or takes over 10 s), the scene runs single-threaded, and development builds (or the `debug` option) warn once per scene why. Bundlers need no setting beyond the headers; they emit the threaded chunk and its `.wasm` (about 988 KB, 372 KB gzipped) into the build output, which single-threaded pages never download. See "Multithreading (COOP/COEP)" in the README for the header setup in Vite, webpack-dev-server, nginx and Express.

- [#44](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/44) [`9d89ed3`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/9d89ed3744839ae212150a0d20a5a566ce0e3c78) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Add `fast-mesh-wasm/three`, a three.js adapter for the `Scene` API. `bindMesh(scene, mesh, options?)` returns a `THREE.Mesh` whose geometry follows the scene through `commitFrame()` (dirty ranges only, colour attribute while colouring is on, `setOpacity`, `dispose`); `attachTransformControls(controls, scene, bound)` turns a TransformControls drag into one scene drag and one undo step; `bindSection(scene, section, bound, options?)` draws a section's contours and fill and clips the bound meshes at its plane (set `renderer.localClippingEnabled = true`). `three` (`>=0.160`) is an optional peer dependency that only this entry imports, and TypeScript users also need `@types/three`. A caller's material is used as given and must not be shared between bound meshes.
  
  Core additions: `DragSession.active` tells whether the drag is still open, `Scene.sectionPlane(section)` returns a section's current plane, and `Scene.removeMesh` now ends an active drag on that mesh (as `undo()` does) instead of leaving it open.
  
  There is also a raw WebGPU example in `examples/webgpu` that uses no 3D library.

- [#47](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/47) [`bddc257`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/bddc2570c88b17138ef37112f83c47d9e1bb29aa) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Add `fast-mesh-wasm/worker` (browsers): `createWorkerScene(options?)` runs the `Scene` engine in a Web Worker and resolves to a `WorkerScene` with the same methods. Handles are returned at once; state changes are fire-and-forget and report progress through `status(key)` / `onStatus`; queries (`raycast`, `closestPoint`, `meshDistance`, `intersects`, `findContacts`, `analyze`, `fetchFieldValues`, `fieldStatus`, `sectionGap`) and `edit` / `repair` return promises. Call `commitFrame()` once per animation frame: it sends the frame's requests as one batch and delivers the change records of the results received, whose dirty ranges were transferred into main-thread mirrors, so `views`, `matrix4`, `placement`, `section` and `sectionPlane` stay synchronous and the three.js adapter works unchanged. Requests run in order; superseded colouring, section and drag-move requests are dropped before they start, and results older than what is shown are ignored. Drags are optimistic (the placement follows the pointer at once; `end` and `cancel` are synchronous). After a trap every call fails with `INSTANCE_POISONED` until `restart()`, which starts a new Worker and replays the meshes, placements, fields, colourings and sections (history is cleared). The Worker script is loaded with `new Worker(new URL("./worker-entry.js", import.meta.url), { type: "module" })`; the `workerUrl` and `createWorker` options override it. The Worker scene is always eager and has no `beginStep` / `endStep` / `cancelStep` or `takeChanges`.
  
  The three.js adapter (`bindMesh`, `bindSection`, `attachTransformControls`) now accepts any `SceneLike`, a new exported interface (`views`, `matrix4`, `placement`, `section`, `sectionPlane`, `onChanges`, `commitFrame`, `drag`) that both `Scene` and `WorkerScene` implement. This is a type-only change: code passing a `Scene` is unaffected.

### Patch Changes

- [#55](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/55) [`df09cc9`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/df09cc99920cc9beb3651fcee44abd22a022019e) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Multithreaded Worker scene: a crash on one of the engine's pool threads no longer hangs the scene. Before, a Rust panic or a WebAssembly trap in work split across threads killed only that thread, and pending promises never settled. Now the scene fails every pending call with `INSTANCE_POISONED` and terminates the Worker, as the single-threaded engine does; `restart()` recovers with a new pool. Pool threads report the crash on a `BroadcastChannel`, so nothing is polled and single-threaded scenes are unchanged. A few failures still cannot be detected (a stack overflow in WebAssembly memory, a Worker the browser ends without an exception, a thread that hangs); see "Crashes on a pool thread" in the README.

- [#56](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/56) [`3392e4a`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/3392e4a190c195aff738bfe78e0c33d50e51b579) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - `SceneStore` refreshes its snapshot incrementally: an action or status change re-derives only the meshes and sections it touches, and scene events in one task make one snapshot. With 1000 meshes, `select` drops from about 310 µs to under 1 µs, a placement from about 340 µs to 14 µs, and a Worker status delivery from about 520 µs to 55 µs. Adding 500 meshes onto 1000 takes 30 ms instead of 203 ms. Actions still notify subscribers synchronously; updates driven by the scene (its `onChanges` and `onStatus` events) now notify one microtask later, and `getSnapshot()` is up to date before then.

- [#60](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/60) [`ae293c4`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/ae293c482e7ed954fa4e089dab7ba8e52856456d) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Multithreaded Worker scene: on WebKit 26 (as in Playwright 1.56's build; Safari 26 may be affected), the scene now starts single-threaded in about 50 ms instead of after the 10 s thread-pool timeout. WebKit 26 traps `memory.fill` and `memory.copy` on a shared memory that another thread grew, so its thread pool could never start. A probe of about 10 ms, run while the threaded glue is imported, now detects this before the glue is instantiated. The verdict is kept for the page, so restarts and later scenes skip the probe. Other browsers are unchanged and keep the threaded engine. See "Older WebKit" in the README.

- [#51](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/pull/51) [`0d7ab20`](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun/fast-mesh-wasm/commit/0d7ab20cb7990264b71930f29516625ad2a4ef88) Thanks [@YoonDongGeun](https://github.com/YoonDongGeun)! - Faster wide-range distance colouring while dragging: when most of a mesh is within a field's `maxDistance`, the per-pose update now recomputes every vertex instead of first listing every nearby triangle pair, which makes a 1M-triangle drag with `maxDistance` 8 about 29 times faster per pose. Values and colours are unchanged, bit for bit. In the Worker scene (`fast-mesh-wasm/worker`), the field recompute after a drag move now runs in small steps, and a recompute whose pose is already stale (a newer move, drag cancel, `setPlacement`, edit, repair, compact, undo/redo, removal of either mesh or removal of the field is queued) is dropped, so colours follow the latest pose without a backlog; during a sustained drag a recompute is kept and shown once 200 ms have passed since the last colours, so they still update while the pointer moves. Each pose's colours are still applied all at once. In Node `worker_threads` the engine now yields with `setImmediate`, so requests sent during a long recompute arrive between its steps. No API change.
